# Wombats

Wombats remain first-class STANKOPEDIA citizens.

A Wombat is a living page, experimental document, institutional oddity, or useful knowledge object that does not belong inside a strict engineering standard but should still be preserved.

## Wombat Rules

1. Wombats are preserved unless explicitly retired.
2. Wombats may become references, standards, registries, or templates later.
3. Wombats are not automatically binding engineering standards.
4. Wombats should be registered when they become operationally useful.

## Registry

The Wombat Registry should identify each Wombat by ID, title, status, and purpose.
