# 🧦 Advanced Sock Physics

The study of sock-based phenomena remains one of the most controversial branches of modern stank science.

While traditional physicists continue to insist that socks obey conventional laws of matter and energy, extensive research conducted by the Department of Advanced Stank suggests otherwise.

---

## The Missing Sock Principle

The First Law of Sock Physics states:

> Any collection of socks will contain one fewer sock than was originally placed into a laundry system.

The missing sock is believed to transition into a parallel dimension known as the **Lint Continuum**.

## Sock Density

Sock Density (SD) is calculated as:

SD = (Age × Stank) / Elasticity

High-density socks can develop gravitational properties capable of attracting nearby laundry.

Every dryer contains a microscopic singularity.

Objects crossing the Dryer Event Horizon become:

- Lost forever
- Found six months later
- Somebody else's problem

Scientists remain divided.

## Quantum Sock Entanglement

Two socks that have shared a drawer become permanently entangled.

When one sock disappears:

- The other becomes lonely.
- The remaining sock becomes impossible to throw away.
- The owner insists the missing sock will return.
