# ☢️ The Funk Field Theory

The Funk Field Theory attempts to explain all known stank phenomena through a single unified field.

Developed by Professor Mustington in 1978, the theory remains the cornerstone of advanced stank research.

---

## Core Principle

The universe is permeated by an invisible substance known as **The Funk Field**.

Every object emits measurable levels of funk.

Examples include:

| Object | Funk Level |
|----------|----------|
| Fresh Laundry | Low |
| Gym Bag | Moderate |
| Forgotten Hoodie | High |
| Mystery Couch Cushion | Critical |

---

## Funk Propagation

Funk naturally flows from regions of high concentration toward regions of low concentration.

This process is known as:

> Aromatic Equalization

Attempts to prevent Funk Propagation have historically failed.

---

## Observable Effects

Exposure to elevated Funk Fields may cause:

- Increased wisdom
- Reduced concern about appearances
- Appreciation for cheese
- Interest in dumpster architecture
- Sudden ownership of multiple hoodies

---

## The Grand Unified Stank

The theory proposes that all known forces are merely expressions of funk:

- Gravity = Heavy Funk
- Magnetism = Directional Funk
- Time = Slow Funk
- Bureaucracy = Concentrated Funk
