# Personal Journal of Dr. Aldus Wombsworth

## Entry 1

### First Day

Today was my first day at Big Dumb Idiot Labs.

The facility is significantly larger than the recruitment materials suggested.

The recruitment materials also neglected to mention the smell.

I have now encountered what senior staff refer to as "background stank."

Apparently one becomes accustomed to it.

I have doubts.

My supervisor, Dr. Grumbles, informed me that science is equal parts observation, perseverance, and paperwork.

The paperwork appears to occupy approximately ninety percent of the equation.

Despite this, I am excited.

I have spent my entire academic life studying anomalous stank dynamics.

This facility houses some of the greatest scientific minds in the world.

I intend to become one of them.

One day I will contribute something meaningful.

One day I will publish findings that matter.

One day I may even earn the Nobel Wombat Prize.

I realize that sounds arrogant.

Still.

A scientist should have ambitions.

Tomorrow I begin my first assignment.

The future appears promising.
