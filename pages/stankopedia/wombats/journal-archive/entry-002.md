# Personal Journal of Dr. Aldus Wombsworth

## Entry 2

### Orientation

Today I attended facility orientation.

I learned several things.

First, there are apparently fourteen departments dedicated exclusively to stank.

Second, there is an active dispute regarding the definition of stank.

Third, nobody agrees with anyone else.

A heated argument broke out between Applied Stank Sciences and Theoretical Stank Sciences.

The discussion lasted three hours.

No conclusions were reached.

One participant bit another participant.

This was apparently not unusual.

The incident required three forms.

I am beginning to understand why the paperwork burden is so severe.

Despite this, I remain optimistic.

The research opportunities here are extraordinary.

There is genuine groundbreaking work being performed within these walls.

At least I assume there is.

I have not located it yet.

Tomorrow I begin laboratory work.

I am eager to prove myself.
