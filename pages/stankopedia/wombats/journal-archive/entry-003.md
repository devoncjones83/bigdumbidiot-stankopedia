# Personal Journal of Dr. Aldus Wombsworth

## Entry 3

### First Experiment

Disaster.

Not a catastrophic disaster.

Merely an embarrassing one.

I spent six weeks preparing a presentation regarding localized stank accumulation patterns.

The model was elegant.

The calculations were rigorous.

The results appeared promising.

Unfortunately, I accidentally reversed two variables.

The model predicted anti-stank.

Several senior researchers found this hilarious.

One asked if I had discovered cleanliness.

I did not appreciate the joke.

Still.

Mistakes are part of science.

The model can be corrected.

The work remains valid.

I will improve.

I must.

One day they will take me seriously.
