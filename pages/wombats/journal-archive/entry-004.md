# Personal Journal of Dr. Aldus Wombsworth

## Entry 4

### Nobel Season

The Nobel Wombat Prize nominations were announced today.

I am not eligible, of course.

My career has barely begun.

Still, I attended the ceremony.

Professor Burrowsworth won for his paper:

"Additional Stank."

The paper consisted of four pages.

Three pages were charts.

The final page simply stated:

"More stank appears to result in additional stank."

The audience applauded.

I applauded as well.

Mostly because everyone else was applauding.

I have spent the evening attempting to understand what I am missing.

Surely the work possesses hidden complexity.

Surely there is brilliance I have failed to appreciate.

I have now read the paper three times.

I am increasingly concerned that there is not.

Perhaps this institution is stranger than I realized.
