# Personal Journal of Dr. Aldus Wombsworth

## Entry 5

### Dr. Grumbles

I spent most of today working alongside Dr. Grumbles.

He has worked at Big Dumb Idiot Labs for forty-one years.

His office contains seventeen filing cabinets.

None appear organized.

When I asked how he located anything, he laughed for nearly a minute.

Then he said:

"Eventually the stank tells you where things are."

I assumed this was a joke.

Several hours later he located a report that had been missing since before I was born.

I am no longer certain it was a joke.

Before leaving, he offered advice.

He said:

"Don't spend your whole life chasing prizes, kid. The prizes never know they're being chased."

At the time I dismissed this as the ramblings of an aging scientist.

I am recording it only because it seemed important to him.

I suspect I will understand it someday.

Not today.

But someday.
